# Field Recording Protocol Template

## Scope

Record conveyor acoustic samples only under the plant’s approved safety procedure. The phone or microphone must be positioned from a safe location. No team member may approach, touch, stop, or repair a moving conveyor unless authorized and protected by site procedures.

## Recording steps

1. Select the correct conveyor in the app.
2. Stand at the approved measurement point.
3. Keep the phone steady and do not obstruct operators or equipment.
4. Record the agreed duration, preferably 10–15 seconds for the first mobile protocol.
5. Record the operating context immediately afterward.
6. If the sample is abnormal, do not declare a fault from sound alone; request authorized inspection.
7. Save the original file and metadata row together.

## Stop conditions

Stop the recording and leave the area if the supervisor, plant alarm, equipment condition, or safety procedure requires it. Mark the sample as incomplete rather than guessing.
